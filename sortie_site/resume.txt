ASSUMPTIONS AND ESTIMATION ACCURACY:
- Wind: ERA5 wind speed at 100 m is reported in km/h. Hourly wind is estimated as the mean of the values at both ends of the hour; hours missing either endpoint are excluded.
- Reference production: mean hourly production divided by installed rated capacity. Negative values are set to zero; missing periods are neither interpolated nor treated as outages.
- Ramp-up: established operation is detected when the 75th percentile of capacity factor is >= 0.90 at wind speeds of at least 43.2 km/h, for at least two consecutive months with at least 10 qualifying hours per month. This threshold is an empirical rule.
- Power curve: median capacity factor in 1.8 km/h wind-speed bins, requiring at least 30 hours per bin; reference-farm values are averaged with weights proportional to hours. Empty bins are interpolated and values are smoothed with a three-bin moving average. The plateau above the last validated bin is an extrapolation, not measured turbine behavior.
- Net power: empirical curve multiplied by a global energy calibration factor (98.0%), then capped at the site's rated capacity. This factor combines outages, curtailment, and model bias; it is not an independent measure of technical availability.
- Capacity factor and equivalent annual energy: capacity factor is mean power divided by rated capacity. Annual energy shown annualizes this mean over 8,766 hours; it is not a measurement of energy produced in a calendar year.
- Monthly panel: each point/bar is the mean over available hours for that calendar month, pooled across all years in the requested period. Power is shown as mean MW, not monthly energy in MWh.
- Apparent time availability: share of hours with wind between 28.8 and 90.0 km/h for which capacity factor exceeds 1.0% of rated capacity. This does not measure machine availability from outage logs.
- The model is most applicable to turbines, hub heights, sites, and conditions represented by the reference farms. Wake effects, hub-height differences, curtailment, and weather-product differences are not corrected separately.
- Measured accuracy from leave-one-farm-out cross-validation: median absolute bias 2.92 capacity-factor percentage points, maximum absolute bias 4.44 points; hourly RMSE 12.9–16.2% of rated capacity; hourly correlation 0.88–0.92.
- Accuracy limitation: validation uses the same farms and periods to tune parameters and assess performance by holding out one farm. It does not measure absolute weather errors and does not guarantee performance for a turbine or site outside these references.
- Sensitivity to reference selection (one farm removed at a time, with no retraining on independent data): net capacity factor 32.35–35.09%, a change of -1.15 to +1.58 percentage points. This range is not a confidence interval.
- Reference-production coverage: 35.0–94.2% across farms; potential bias from missing hours cannot be quantified without knowing their cause.
- Interpretation: displayed decimal places reflect numerical precision, not guaranteed accuracy. Cross-validation and sensitivity above describe performance observed on the references only; they are not a confidence interval for a new site.
