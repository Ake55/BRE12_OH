Farm production

Annual production is the sum of hourly MW values, giving MWh. It uses the same clean production data as the farm pages.

Local consumption

Residents' consumption is estimated from commune population and an average of 2.223 MWh per person per year.

Coverage

Coverage is farm production divided by residents' consumption over the same period. Partial production years and missing hours are noted.
