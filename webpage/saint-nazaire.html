Daily and seasonal patterns

Average output through the day and the year

These charts average measured production for each hour of the day and each month of the selected year. Production is shown on the left axis and average wind speed on the right axis. Only hours with data are averaged; missing periods are left as gaps rather than treated as zero.

Average by hour of day

Shows the typical daily cycle: average MW produced at each hour, alongside the average wind speed for that hour. Each point is a mean over the hours that actually have data for that period. Missing periods are gaps, not zeroes.

Average by month

Shows seasonal variation: average MW produced in each month, alongside the average wind speed for that month. Each point is a mean over the hours that actually have data for that period. Missing periods are gaps, not zeroes.

Averages for the selected year. Partial years appear as gaps.

Production filter

A clean hour needs both T1 and T2 readings and a total inside [−10, 500] MW. Missing values are never replaced by zero.

Wind unit

Wind speed is assumed to be in km/h and is divided by 3.6 to obtain m/s. The source column is kept unchanged on disk.

Hourly join

Production and wind are matched by UTC timestamp, one row to one row, without interpolation. No other farm is added to this one.
