Warnings

    Method and limits

    The curve is the median capacity factor per 0.5 m/s wind bin, combined across the reference farms with hour weighting. Ramp-up is excluded from each farm. The energy calibration factor is the ratio of observed to modelled reference production. It is not a technical availability measure.

    ERA5 wind is a gridded reanalysis with known offshore biases. The reference turbines are 6–8 MW units, so a very different turbine size changes the real curve. Wake losses are implicitly included in the reference data but no layout-specific correction is applied. Beyond the validated wind range, the curve is an extrapolation.
